# Goldwright changelog

## 0.6.0

Play with a controller, and match ElvUI or EllesmereUI.

- **Controller:** in the game's Gamepad mode, Goldwright's windows now work with the controller. RT and LT move into Goldwright's window, the D-pad moves between its buttons, A presses them (posting included) and B closes the window. During combat Goldwright steps aside and comes back after the fight. `/gw gamepad` shows what the controller support sees.
- **ElvUI and EllesmereUI:** with either installed, Goldwright's windows, buttons, tabs and boxes take its look. Prefer the standard look? With ElvUI, turn off "Match ElvUI's look" in Goldwright's Settings and `/reload`; with EllesmereUI, turn Goldwright off under its third-party add-on skins.
- **Scan:** the Scan tab now says a full scan takes 30–60 seconds. The auction house has grown since launch week, when it took about 18.
